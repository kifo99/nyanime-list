
# Nyanime-List Social Platform for anime lovers

Nyanime List is a social platform designed for anime lovers to connect, share, and explore the anime world together. Users can track the anime they've watched or plan to watch, write reviews, and create custom anime lists. The platform also allows users to add friends, chat with them, and see shared reviews directly in their feed. Whether you're a casual viewer or a dedicated otaku, Nyanime List helps you express your opinions, discover new shows, and bond with fellow fans.


## Features

- **Anime Tracking:** Add anime to your watched, watching, or plan to watch lists.
- **Reviews & Ratings:** Write detailed reviews and rate anime you've seen.
- **Custom Lists:** Organize anime into personalized lists like “Top 10,” “Seasonal Picks,” or any theme you choose.
- **Friends & Social Feed:** Add friends, follow their activity, and see their shared reviews in your feed.
- **Review Sharing Control:** Choose whether to share each review to your feed or keep it on your profile only.
- **Chat System:** Message your friends and discuss anime directly within the platform.


## Tech Stack

**Front-End:** React, Tailwind, Zustand, React Query 

**Back-end:** Node, Express, MongoDB
